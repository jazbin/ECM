## Can body — required piecewise split (detail)

Per `CAN_CAP_REDUCTION_DECISION.md` (EXACT PIECEWISE UNION SUPPORTED), the single BDS `Can` solid must be split at z_of = 65.3413mm (JR-aligned) into two material zones if STAR supports piecewise assignment within one body:

- **z_of < 65.3413mm** (lower 92% of the body by length, containing the 9.67%-by-volume true Can-domain material plus the 83.7% that intersects the *OpenFOAM JR reference domain* — see T06_GEOMETRIC_EQUIVALENCE_AUDIT.md): Can properties (ρ=8000, c_p=500, k=16 isotropic) for the true Can-domain sub-volume; **the OF-JR-domain-intersecting sub-volume within this same solid must NOT get Can properties** — it must get JR properties, because OpenFOAM treats that spatial zone as JellyRoll, not steel. This means the Can solid cannot be assigned a single material even within the Can-side split; it needs a **second** internal split isolating the OF-JR-domain-coincident portion.
- **z_of ≥ 65.3413mm** (top 8%): Cap properties (ρ=1447.2, c_p=500, k=(0.01,0.01,0.1)).
- **z_of < 0mm** (bottom hardware region, 3.2% by volume + the associated bottom EndPlate/Washer/Post/Tab bodies): no OF domain exists here at all — see resolution options below.

**Dimensional caveat on the 3-way zone count:** the above analysis was performed at T06 radial dimensions (Can ID = 18.000 mm, JR OD = 17.881 mm). At the final radial target (Can ID = JR OD = 20.627 mm), the corrected Can wall will no longer occupy the OF JR reference domain — the "JR-equivalent inner zone" of the Can body shrinks toward zero as Can ID approaches the OF JR OD. The number of required material sub-zones for the Can body is therefore final-radial-geometry-dependent. The axial zone structure (Can-span vs Cap-zone vs bottom-outside) may still require a split even at final radial dimensions and depends on final axial geometry. Do not treat the current 3-way split as a topology invariant — re-evaluate after RAD corrections establish final radial geometry.

In practice this means the BDS `Can` body may require **two or three** distinct thermal zones depending on final radial dimensions, not necessarily always three. This is an unresolved STAR capability question (STAR-015) gated on final geometry and must be tested at that time (Test A in the qualification plan).

## Resolution options for the bottom-end ("outside-reference") bodies

The governing task defines six possible resolution categories (A–F). Applied here:

- **Option A (thermally split the BDS body at the OF domain boundary)**: not applicable in the same way as the Can body — there is no OF domain to split into on the bottom end; splitting doesn't create a target.
- **Option B (piecewise material within one body)**: not applicable for the same reason.
- **Option C (electrical geometry retained but thermally suppressed)**: plausible — assign the bottom Tab Stem/Washer/EndPlate/Internal-Post bodies a thermal material designed to reproduce the *aggregate* effect of OF's single thin resistive layer (i.e., make the whole stack behave, in bulk axial resistance, like the OF `thicknessLayers=6.015e-7m` contact layer) while leaving their electrical role untouched. This requires computing an equivalent lumped resistance for the stack and is the most promising option, but is a **derived approximation**, not an exact mapping, and must be labeled as such.
- **Option D (equivalent contact/interface treatment)**: closely related to C — insert an explicit interface thermal resistance between Can and Jellyroll at the bottom equal to OF's contact resistance, and suppress/insulate the intervening hardware (Internal-Post, Washer, Tab) so they do not provide a parallel low-resistance path. This directly targets Key Finding 3 in the geometric audit (the confirmed Can→EndPlate→Post→Washer→TabStem→TabRoot→Jellyroll contact chain).
- **Option E (different BDS geometry)**: not pursued this run (governing task says not to regenerate client geometry unnecessarily).
- **Option F (approximate reduced-order calibration)**: fallback if C/D cannot be validated in STAR.

**Recommendation for the client test plan: attempt D first** (explicit interface resistance + suppression of the metallic bottom shortcut), since it directly targets the confirmed conduction-chain finding, is the closest in spirit to "no artificial geometry change," and is testable as a bounded STAR configuration question rather than a curve-fit.

## Volume-coverage caveat (from the capacitance audit) — DIMENSIONAL FINDING, not decision-driving

`THERMAL_CAPACITANCE_AUDIT.csv` shows the OF Can and Cap domains are only 56.1% and 15.9% covered by any BDS solid material at all. **This is an expected geometry mismatch, not evidence of an architectural BDS thermal-mass deficiency.** T06 (`T06_TARGET_AXIAL_SURPLUS_2p00.step`) was built at the previous 19.25mm JR OD radial target, while the OF reference domains used for this comparison are sized to the final 20.6274mm JR OD = Can ID target (`NEXTSESSION`, 2026-09-10; `tbm_validation/OPENFOAM_ECM_EQUIVALENCE_TARGET.md`). Comparing a smaller-radius body against larger-radius reference domains necessarily shows apparent "missing" volume — that is arithmetic, not a finding about BDS's capability to represent thermal mass. The same class of gap is already tracked, independent of this Can/Cap check, by the project's ongoing JR-OD clearance work (`NEXTSESSION`).

Do not treat 56.1%/15.9% coverage or the ~44%/84% undershoot figures below as inputs to a STAR test plan or a claim about BDS architecture. They must be recomputed against a final-radial-dimension T06/T-series or H-series export before they mean anything decision-relevant. See `docs/equivalence/T06_GEOMETRIC_EQUIVALENCE_AUDIT.md`'s "Finding classification" section for the full topological-vs-dimensional split.

(Original wording, retained for record: even with perfect piecewise assignment, the *mapped* thermal capacitance in the Can/Cap regions will undershoot the OF reference by ~44% and ~84% respectively at T06's current, non-final radial dimensions.)

## Overall answer: can an exact thermal mapping be constructed?

**CONDITIONAL.** JellyRoll (Mandrel+Jellyroll, 100% exact) and the top/+Ve hardware (100% exact subdivision of Cap) map exactly today. The Can body requires a piecewise material split (STAR-015; number of zones is final-radial-geometry-dependent — the "JR-equivalent inner zone" was derived at T06 dimensions and may not be required at final radial target where Can ID = JR OD). The bottom/−Ve hardware has no OF-domain target at all and requires an approximation (Option D recommended) rather than an exact reduction. The topological findings above (Can crosses OF reference domains, bottom hardware has no OF target, confirmed metallic contact-chain shortcut) hold regardless of final dimensions and are the basis for this CONDITIONAL rating — the ~44–84% coverage numbers do not factor into it and must not be cited as a reason for CONDITIONAL vs. some other rating. Note: "crosses JR/Can/Cap domains" refers to the BDS Can solid intersecting OpenFOAM reference spatial domains, NOT to the BDS Can body physically overlapping the BDS Jellyroll body (confirmed 0.059504 mm radial gap between those BDS bodies).
