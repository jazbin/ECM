Implicit vs explicit coupling issues encountered (solids-only multi-region)

Context
- Case: `cases/lumped_solid` (solids-only, multi-region) with ECM volumetric heating.
- When `useImplicit true` at solid-solid interfaces, temperatures undershot below the initial 313.15 K despite positive volumetric heat input.
- The Tmin drop was observed after `correctThermos`, consistent with fvMatrixAssembly behavior in the implicit path.

Symptoms
- Non-physical interface cooling with implicit coupling enabled in solids-only runs.
- Tmin drop appeared after `correctThermos` even with positive `ecmQdot`.
- Explicit coupling (`useImplicit false`) did not show the undershoot in short runs and produced physically consistent temperature evolution.

Mitigations and current state
- Forced explicit coupling for solids-only runs when `useImplicit true` is detected (guard added in `chtMultiRegionSolidFoam`).
- Added `allowImplicitSolidsOnly` override for experimental implicit runs.
- Added `debugImplicitCoupling` instrumentation around `fvMatrixAssembly` to localize the Tmin drop.
- Added `implicitClampTMin` (in `fvSolution`) to prevent non-physical undershoot during implicit runs; this is a stopgap, not a root-cause fix.

Evidence / logs
- Implicit undershoot observed (solids-only): `artifacts/logs/lumped_run_20260325_195021.log`.
- Tmin drop after `correctThermos`: `artifacts/logs/lumped_run_20260325_204137.log`.
- Clamp stabilizes Tmin in implicit mode: `artifacts/logs/lumped_run_20260325_210754.log`.
- Long implicit run with clamp: `artifacts/logs/lumped_run_20260325_211539.log`.
- Explicit coupling fixes interface cooling in solids-only case: `artifacts/logs/lumped_run_20260325_132444.log`.

Open questions
- Root cause in `fvMatrixAssembly` for solids-only implicit coupling remains unresolved.
- Need a minimal reproducer to isolate whether this is mesh-specific, interface BC-specific, or a solver-level artifact.
