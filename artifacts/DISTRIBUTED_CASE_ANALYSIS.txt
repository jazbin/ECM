KEY OBSERVATIONS
1. Different solver types: Lumped uses unsteady SolidFoam (explicit transient),
   Distributed uses SimpleFoam (steady-state iterative)

2. Heat generation profiles differ significantly:
   - Lumped: Maintains ~90W for entire duration (lumped ECM profile)
   - Distributed: Starts high (63.8W) then decays to 23.8W

3. Mesh resolution trade-off:
   - Distributed has fewer cells (27k vs 50k-260k) but uses steady-state solver
   - This reduces temporal accuracy but speeds execution

4. Spatial coupling validation:
   - Zone weights sum to 0.00911 m³ = 100% of jellyRoll volume
   - Equal partition confirms proper zone distribution
